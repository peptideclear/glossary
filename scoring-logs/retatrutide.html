Note: Retatrutide's TRIUMPH-1 result (28.3% mean body weight reduction at 80 weeks on 12mg; 30.3% at 104 weeks in the BMI ≥35 extension subgroup) is the largest weight loss reported in a Phase 3 obesity trial to date, exceeding tirzepatide's SURMOUNT-1 result. The evidence grade and RQS band are Moderate rather than Strong because the full peer-reviewed Phase 3 publication is pending, independent replication does not yet exist, and the broader TRIUMPH program is still reading out. Funding Independence remains the structural ceiling on this score — all evidence is Lilly-sponsored.

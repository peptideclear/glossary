Note: In the published TRIUMPH-1 paper, the highest dose produced a 25.0% mean body weight reduction at 80 weeks versus 3.9% on placebo, counting all randomized participants (treatment-regimen estimand). Lilly's topline efficacy estimate, which assumes participants took the drug as intended, was 28.3%. This is the largest weight loss reported in a Phase 3 obesity trial to date, though no head-to-head comparison with other agents has reported yet. The score now falls in the Strong Evidence band on rubric math. Note that the Strong band describes evidence that has been independently replicated, which retatrutide's has not: all evidence remains Lilly-sponsored, and Funding Independence and Replication remain the structural ceiling on this score. PeptideClear applies the rubric identically across compounds rather than adjusting bands by judgment. The band is a mechanical result, not an endorsement.

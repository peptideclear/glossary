Note: This is the load-bearing caveat for the profile — Gonadorelin has genuinely strong, decades-deep clinical evidence, but specifically for FDA-approved indications (GnRH stimulation testing, ovulation induction in hypothalamic amenorrhea) and CHH fertility restoration. The popular off-label TRT-adjunct use case (preserving testicular function during testosterone therapy) has comparatively limited direct trial evidence as of April 2026 — the CHH population (intact, unstimulated pituitary) is mechanistically distinct from TRT patients, whose pituitary is actively suppressed by exogenous testosterone. This mirrors the HGH precedent on this site: score the approved/established use, caveat the off-label community application explicitly.

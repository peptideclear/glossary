Note: Kisspeptin-10 acts one step upstream of GnRH in the reproductive hormone cascade rather than mimicking GnRH directly — a meaningful mechanistic distinction from Gonadorelin. Its strongest evidence is in IVF oocyte-maturation triggering as a physiological alternative to hCG, where it shows a more favorable safety profile against ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome. The community-discussed use as a TRT-adjunct for testicular preservation has comparatively thinner direct evidence than the reproductive-medicine literature this score reflects. Kisspeptin-10 also occupies an unsettled regulatory position following a negative FDA Advisory Committee vote in late 2024 — see the Live Regulatory Tracker for updates.
